# Teloi to Physical Scope Projection Contract

## Purpose and ownership

Every Build Bible scope SHALL be evaluated against the applicable upstream Teloi before a physical design is promoted. Teloi answer **why**, the Build Bible answers **what physical state and capability are required**, implementation records answer **how**, and reality/evidence answers **what actually exists**.

This is a generic public physical-design contract. The normative goals, personal preferences and standing Teloi registry are owned by their upstream Polaris/Wayfinder source. Private property-specific translations live with the authorized private project source, not in this public repository. Do not copy personal circumstances, household records or private goals into this standard.

## Required translation

telos -> desired state -> protected constraint -> physical function -> capability -> geometry/interface/capacity -> candidate mechanism -> implementation decision -> verification -> observed outcome.

Do not confuse requirement, function, mechanism and implementation. An aesthetic, brand, policy preference or technology must not become a structural requirement without a traceable causal pathway.

Every applicable pair `(canonical_telos_id, physical_scope_id)` SHALL receive one auditable disposition:

1. `requirement`: changes a physical performance or use requirement.
2. `constraint`: protects a physical, legal, safety, privacy, cost or human-use boundary.
3. `interface`: demands a location, clearance, conduit, service, replacement, data, utility, egress or expansion interface.
4. `acceptance_condition`: changes evidence needed to call the physical state successful.
5. `context_only`: informs selection but adds no separate build requirement.
6. `no_physical_delta`: reviewed and does not affect that scope; include reason when consequential.

These are **dispositions**, not six independent new Teloi. A scope must never receive a fabricated improvement project merely because a Telos appears upstream.

## Recursive scopes

Apply the contract to property -> campus/shared infrastructure -> site zone -> building -> floor/wing -> room -> system -> assembly -> component/interface. Child scopes inherit relevant requirements and constraints. Shared requirements retain one canonical owner; children link instead of copying numeric facts.

Named structure scopes (such as a Guest House, Main House, greenhouse and workshop) remain independent. A house requirement cannot be silently imposed on a workshop. A Guest House circulation choice cannot silently become a Main House circulation constraint.

## Record fields

- `telos_id` and source revision/path
- `physical_scope_id`, parent and affected population
- `disposition` and why it applies
- `requirement_id` / `interface_id` / `acceptance_id` if relevant
- `protected_constraints`, authority, code/engineering gates
- `evidence_state`, provenance and confidence
- `expected_benefit`, lifecycle costs, displaced labor, externalities and reversibility when material
- `acceptance_test`, observed result and result status
- `conflict_ids`, alternatives, scope owner, revision and supersession

Preserve goal, preference, assumption, candidate, decision, procurement, installation, commissioning, measured reality and supersession as distinct epistemic states.

## Constitutional invariants

- **Reality:** evidence must establish site/legal/physical feasibility before construction claims become verified.
- **Agency and legitimacy:** local controls, accessibility, human authority, consent, external-service access and legal/safety requirements are not disposable.
- **Peace and flourishing:** daily life, rest, quiet, nature, family, craft and hospitality are the purpose; automation and infrastructure are subordinate servants.
- **Stewardship:** account for resources, maintenance, biological effects, externalities, reuse and actual lifecycle burden.
- **Continuity:** preserve replaceability, manual/degraded operation, independent failure domains, and useful successor-state documentation.
- **Typed measurement:** cash vs debt, stock vs flow, energy vs power, planned vs realized savings, recurring vs capital cost, and observed vs estimated effects stay distinct.
- **Tradeoffs:** no invented single numerical Telos score; conflicting hard constraints are surfaced and independently evaluated.

## Evidence and conformance

1. Enumerate the current upstream Teloi (do not rely on a stale snapshot) and prove every applicable ID has a disposition for the requested review scope.
2. Reject missing critical evidence, unsafe unbounded failures, illegal dependencies, unresolved conflict and authorization gaps.
3. Compare materially distinct feasible designs by function, lifecycle burden, uncertainty, reversibility and actual user outcome.
4. Accept engineering-dependent items only after applicable licensed design, permits, construction inspections and commissioned performance evidence.
5. Run a living reality check after occupation and use results to update successor designs. A Guest House prototype does not prove a Main House design without appropriate transfer evidence.
6. Invalidate only materially affected downstream decisions when a Telos, law, reality state, or requirement changes.
