# Resource Allocation and ATR

Wayfinder does not optimize a single scalar objective. Resource allocation remains
subordinate to mission, Teloi, constraints, Bearings, and useful future options.

## Actionable Time Redirection

Actionable Time Redirection (ATR) redirects reclaimed capacity — time, attention,
energy, maintenance burden, uncertainty, and similar resources — into higher-value
capability.

ATR is not merely minutes saved. Eliminating recurring checks, context switches,
decisions, maintenance, or rediscovery can create more useful capacity than a larger
nominal time saving.

ATR may be produced by removing avoidable burden, multiplied through reuse and
standards, preserved by preventing maintenance debt, and converted into durable
capability.

## Priority Cascade

The Priority Cascade allocates available capacity among justified candidate actions.
It is an allocator, not the source of values.

Mission / Teloi
-> reality and constraints
-> Bearings
-> candidate actions
-> consequence, uncertainty, reversibility, cost, and dependencies
-> Priority Cascade
-> bounded execution.

No score or efficiency proxy may override a vision-critical requirement.

## ATR Flywheel

Observe reality
-> reduce consequential uncertainty
-> remove avoidable burden
-> create ATR
-> allocate ATR
-> strengthen capability
-> expand affordances
-> expose further useful opportunities.

The loop is descriptive, not compulsory. Wayfinder may intentionally spend more
effort where that improves experience, craftsmanship, resilience, learning,
meaning, or another established objective.

## Related Concepts

- **Leverage** describes compounding effects; it is not a separate subsystem.
- **Expected Uncertainty Reduction (EUR)** compares the value of reducing
  consequential uncertainty against cost, attention, risk, and reversibility.
- **Capability Harvest** can create ATR by replacing commodity work with maintained
  capability.

High EUR does not automatically mean high priority. The uncertainty must matter to
a real decision, capability, commitment, Telos, or path.

ATR, savings, efficiency, utilization, and similar measures are Bearings, not goals
to maximize indefinitely. Healthy-region navigation applies.

Capability Harvest remains subject to Vision Supremacy. Work saved at the cost of
required behavior, sovereignty, continuity, identity, or architecture is not a gain.
