# Resource Allocation, ATR, and Priority Cascade

Wayfinder does not optimize a single scalar objective. Resource allocation remains
subordinate to mission, Teloi, constraints, Bearings, and preservation of useful
future options.

## Actionable Time Redirection

Actionable Time Redirection (ATR) is the redirection of reclaimed resources — time,
attention, energy, maintenance burden, uncertainty, and other released capacity —
into higher-value capability.

ATR is not merely "minutes saved." Eliminating many recurring checks, context
switches, decisions, or maintenance loops may create more useful capacity than a
larger block of nominal time savings.

A system may therefore:

- produce ATR by removing unnecessary labor or uncertainty;
- multiply ATR by improving reuse, standards, knowledge, automation, or recovery;
- preserve ATR by preventing maintenance debt and repeated rediscovery; and
- convert ATR into durable capability.

## Priority Cascade

The Priority Cascade allocates available attention, effort, money, time, and other
reclaimed capacity toward the most consequential justified work.

It is an allocator, not the source of values. It must receive mission/Telos
constraints and current Bearings before ranking candidate actions.

Preferred logic:

Mission / Teloi
-> current reality and constraints
-> Bearings and Color States
-> candidate actions / opportunities
-> consequence, uncertainty, reversibility, cost, and dependency review
-> Priority Cascade
-> bounded execution.

No score or economic proxy may silently override a vision-critical requirement.

## ATR Flywheel

A useful compounding loop is:

Observe reality
-> reduce consequential uncertainty
-> remove avoidable burden
-> create ATR
-> allocate ATR through the Priority Cascade
-> build or strengthen capability
-> expand future affordances
-> create further opportunities for uncertainty and burden reduction.

This flywheel is descriptive, not compulsory. Wayfinder may intentionally spend
more time or effort where doing so increases experience, craftsmanship, resilience,
meaning, learning, or another established objective.

## Leverage Reconciliation

Older material sometimes compressed ATR, automation value, standards, knowledge,
capability growth, and the Priority Cascade into the word "Leverage."

Leverage remains a useful descriptive lens but is not a separate canonical
architectural primitive. Use the more precise concepts:

- ATR for reclaimed-and-redirected capacity;
- Priority Cascade for allocation;
- Capability for durable ability;
- Affordance Projection for newly possible action; and
- Bearings for navigation.

This avoids turning a broad metaphor into another duplicate subsystem.

## Expected Uncertainty Reduction

EUR complements ATR. EUR asks which intervention removes the most consequential
uncertainty relative to cost, time, attention, risk, and reversibility. ATR asks
what useful capacity becomes available when burdens are removed and where that
capacity can be redirected.

High EUR does not automatically imply high priority. The uncertainty must matter to
a real decision, capability, commitment, Telos, or path.

## Anti-Goodhart Boundary

ATR, savings, efficiency, utilization, financial freedom, or any other resource
metric is a Bearing, not an objective to maximize indefinitely.

Healthy-region navigation applies. Excessive optimization can create brittleness,
hoarding, surveillance, churn, joyless efficiency, or loss of maneuverability.

## Capability Harvest Relationship

Capability Harvest is one strong ATR mechanism: mature upstream capability can
eliminate commodity work and redirect effort into unique Wayfinder/Basecamp/Jarvis
needs. It remains subject to Vision Supremacy. Saving work is not valuable when the
price is surrendering required behavior, sovereignty, continuity, or identity.
