# Independent Continental Mobility

Status: Telos / doctrine
Canonical semantic owner: Wayfinder

## Telos

Preserve and increase a person's practical ability to travel continental distances on their own terms.

The protected capability is **independent mobility**, not any particular drivetrain, fuel, vehicle class, or infrastructure mechanism.

## Preferred state region

A person can:

- depart spontaneously without unnecessary permission or account dependence;
- choose destination and route and change either en route;
- carry people and ordinary personal cargo;
- travel continental distances at sustainable household cost;
- replenish propulsion energy through broadly available, interoperable infrastructure;
- retain meaningful movement privacy and avoid unnecessary detailed travel records;
- own/control the mobility endpoint and retain practical independent repair options;
- continue useful operation through network, cloud, payment, or infrastructure degradation where physically practical;
- recover from foreseeable vehicle, energy, route, and infrastructure failures without catastrophic loss of mobility.

## Evaluation dimensions

All metrics must preserve monotonic semantics: higher values mean more of the named property.

- `range_capability`
- `affordability`
- `route_freedom`
- `departure_spontaneity`
- `energy_access_availability`
- `energy_access_interoperability`
- `movement_privacy`
- `user_control`
- `repairability`
- `degraded_mode_capability`
- `failure_recoverability`
- `network_independence`
- `account_independence`

No single dimension is a proxy for the telos. Preserve tradeoffs and protected constraints rather than collapsing them into an unqualified scalar score.

## Mechanism neutrality

ICE, hybrids, battery EVs, synthetic or biofuels, hydrogen, future propulsion systems, roads, ferries, rail interfaces, charging, fueling, and successor infrastructure are mechanisms. Evaluate them by the state they produce across the dimensions above.

A transition is favorable under this telos only to the extent that it preserves or improves the practical mobility state. Improvements on one dimension do not erase material losses on another.

## Privacy and authority

Movement should not require unnecessary surveillance, persistent cloud connectivity, proprietary accounts, remotely revocable feature access, or collection/sale of detailed travel histories. Necessary safety, legal, payment, and infrastructure functions should use the minimum sufficient data and authority consistent with their actual requirements.

## Resilience

Prefer graceful degradation, interoperable replenishment, local/offline capability, multiple viable routes and energy sources where practical, repair/recovery paths, and avoidance of single points of compulsory dependence.

## System integration

This telos composes with, but does not duplicate:

- privacy and data-sovereignty doctrine;
- decentralization and home-first service direction;
- transport and infrastructure resilience;
- household economic equilibrium;
- Physical Polaris ownership/control and degraded-mode principles;
- Reality/Spatial Fabric route, access, distance, and infrastructure observations;
- optionality and continuity doctrine.

Those systems own their reusable primitives. This doctrine supplies the mobility-specific preferred state region and evaluation context.
