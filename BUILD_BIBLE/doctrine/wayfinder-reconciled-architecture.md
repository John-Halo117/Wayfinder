# Wayfinder Reconciled Architecture

This document is the reconciliation map for Wayfinder doctrine. It does not replace
the canonical homes of individual concepts. It establishes precedence, ownership,
and composition so recovered historical concepts strengthen the current system
without recreating parallel architectures.

## Governing Order

When concepts or implementations appear to conflict, resolve them in this order:

1. Mission and established Teloi.
2. Reality and evidence.
3. Constitutional constraints and human authority.
4. Required capability and continuity.
5. Bearings and current navigational consequence.
6. Architecture and Membrane boundaries.
7. Resource allocation and Priority Cascade.
8. Harvested implementation candidates.
9. Minimal bespoke implementation for the irreducible gap.

No lower layer may redefine a higher one merely because it is easier to implement.

## Canonical System Identity

Wayfinder is a mission-first navigation system grounded in reality.

Basecamp is the sovereign composition layer for the physical and digital capability
estate.

Jarvis is the singular human-facing navigator and intelligence identity.

ARK preserves observations, evidence, lineage, constraints, topology, and events.

Capsules preserve re-entry capability and continuity projections.

Harvested applications and protocols provide replaceable capability organs.

No wholesale application becomes Wayfinder, Basecamp, or Jarvis by accumulation of
features.

## Canonical Architecture Chain

Reality
-> observation and evidence
-> CivPhys/mechanics where explanatory value exists
-> constitutional principles and Membranes
-> mission/Teloi and root objectives
-> capabilities and continuity
-> state estimation
-> Color State and epistemic certainty
-> Bearings
-> affordances, constraints, and candidate actions
-> Priority Cascade / resource allocation
-> bounded execution
-> changed reality.

The runtime continuity loop may traverse these layers repeatedly; the repository
structure does not need to mirror the runtime loop one-for-one.

## Root Objectives and Teloi

The recovered root objectives remain useful constitutional lenses:

- Capability — what can we do?
- Continuity — what survives and can be resumed?
- Attention — what still requires the human?
- Maneuverability — what useful future paths remain available?

They do not replace the established Teloi registry. Teloi express the mission's
actual desired directions; root objectives are cross-cutting qualities used to
judge architecture and action across those directions.

Maneuverability remains a long-horizon governor, but optionality is not an excuse
for permanent indecision. Healthy-region navigation applies.

## Bearings and Color State Reconciliation

Bearings are the canonical navigation layer. Metrics are evidence or derived
orientation aids beneath Bearings, not objectives above them.

Operational Color State is:

- Blue — surplus/opportunity;
- Green — healthy/on course;
- Yellow — attention/pressure;
- Red — critical/failure.

Epistemic state is separate:

- White — adequately established, normally implicit;
- Gray — uncertain, stale, conflicting, or low-confidence;
- Black — unknown/unresolved.

Historical uses of Black as an operational existential/severity band are retired.
Black now means epistemic unknown only. Existential operational consequence is
represented by sufficiently severe Red plus the appropriate confidence state.

Color ranks may provide 1–5 ordinal resolution within Blue/Green/Yellow/Red, but
the color meaning remains primary and percentages remain derived.

## Black State Reconciliation

The historical term Black State is retained only as an alias for the epistemic
condition in which a responsible operational Bearing cannot presently be
established. It is not a fifth operational color.

Unknown is valid. The correct response may be to accept the unknown, reduce it when
EUR justifies the effort, or preserve options until evidence improves.

## CivPhys Reconciliation

CivPhys survives as an explanatory mechanics lens using Potential, Pressure, Flow,
and Membrane. It does not become a second ontology or world model.

Reality and evidence remain primary. CivPhys may explain gradients, constraints,
resource movement, or boundary effects after evidence is established.

Asset in Context is a contextual projection over canonical entities and evidence,
not a new entity superclass that duplicates domain models.

## Membranes, TIPS, and Authority

Membrane is the canonical boundary primitive controlling permitted flows of data,
authority, events, state, errors, capability, retention, and disclosure.

Adapters translate. Membranes govern.

TIPS-class trust/authority mechanisms implement or evaluate Membrane policy but do
not replace the Membrane abstraction.

Authority is scoped and relational, not an intrinsic permanent property of an
identity. Prefer explicit participant-capability-resource-membrane-time scopes over
broad global "admin" semantics.

Blackwall is reconciled as a high-assurance Membrane policy profile, not a parallel
security architecture.

Temporary cross-domain reasoning uses Context Bridges with independently bounded
retrieval, reasoning, retention, and disclosure. The ability to correlate data does
not imply permission to retain the correlation forever.

## Capsules and Continuity Reconciliation

Capsules are sedimentary continuity projections. They accrete from evidence,
decisions, artifacts, constraints, outcomes, and unresolved work, then compact into
re-entry surfaces.

They do not compete with ARK for reality ownership and do not compete with Jarvis
for navigation ownership.

ARK answers what happened and what evidence exists. Capsules answer what must be
recoverable to continue. Jarvis answers what matters now and where to steer.

The canonical Capsule maturity sequence is Sediment -> Emerging -> Operational ->
Autonomous, with demotion possible when evidence or continuity value degrades.

## Ambient Certainty, Bearings Economy, and EUR

These remain first-class navigation economics:

- Ambient Certainty is the production of useful certainty without repeated human
  checking.
- Bearings Economy accounts for the attention cost of maintaining orientation.
- Expected Uncertainty Reduction (EUR) estimates the value of removing
  consequential uncertainty relative to cost, attention, risk, and reversibility.

They are complementary, not competing metrics.

## Affordance Projection and Opportunity

Wayfinder asks both "what changed?" and "what became possible?"

Affordance Projection derives newly reachable action from changed reality or
capability. Opportunity Detection filters those affordances through alignment,
actionability, affordability/reachability, relevance, and mission consequence.

Serendipity is allowed as affordance discovery. Novelty is not automatically
entitled to attention.

## ATR and Priority Cascade Reconciliation

Actionable Time Redirection (ATR) accounts for reclaimed capacity redirected into
higher-value capability. It may include time, attention, energy, maintenance
burden, uncertainty, and other released resources.

The Priority Cascade allocates available capacity among candidate actions. It does
not define mission value and may not overrule Teloi, constitutional limits,
Bearings, or vision-critical requirements.

"Leverage" remains a useful descriptive word for compounding effects but is not a
separate canonical subsystem. Use ATR, Priority Cascade, Capability, Affordance,
and Bearings when precision matters.

## Capability Harvest Reconciliation

Capability Harvest searches outward for mature implementation mechanics so
Wayfinder does not rebuild commodity capability.

The order remains:

Vision -> required capability -> architecture -> harvest candidate -> fit test.

Upstream capability displaces bespoke work only when it satisfies the required
behavior, boundaries, continuity, observability, serviceability, and rollback
needs. The existence of a product is not sufficient evidence of fit.

The preferred outcome is often a small unique Wayfinder layer over mature
commodity capability rather than wholesale product adoption.

## Jarvis Reconciliation

Jarvis is not defined by any one UI, model, agent framework, workflow engine,
smart-home platform, database, or "Jarvis" project.

Jarvis owns human-facing identity, continuity of interaction, Bearings,
cross-domain reasoning, attention policy, permission-aware composition, and course
recommendation.

Specialized systems own mature implementation mechanics. Jarvis may harvest their
voice, memory, routing, automation, computer-use, retrieval, notification,
perception, or execution primitives without adopting their worldview.

Jarvis operating modes — Ambient, Conversation, Whiteboard, Mission, Debug, and
Socratic — change interaction posture, not constitutional authority.

## Architecture Maintenance Reconciliation

Architectural Pressure identifies where responsibilities want to move.
Architectural Viscosity identifies why safe movement is difficult.
Architecture Composting preserves durable lessons before obsolete machinery is
deleted.
Architecture Homeostasis continuously detects drift from healthy boundaries.
Architecture Ratchets prevent solved hard debt from silently returning.

WET Under Uncertainty prevents premature unification. An AHA Transition occurs when
evidence finally proves a shared invariant and supports migration into one
canonical path.

These are maintenance mechanics, not new product layers.

## Canonical Compression

Reality is preserved before narrative.
Capabilities are preserved across substrates.
Capsules preserve the ability to continue.
Bearings preserve orientation.
Membranes preserve legitimate boundaries.
Jarvis preserves a singular human-facing course.
Capability Harvest removes commodity work without surrendering vision.
Wayfinder converts evidence and uncertainty into finite Bearings, preserves useful
future options, and directs reclaimed capacity toward durable capability.
