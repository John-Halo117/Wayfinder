# CivPhys, Membranes, and Authority

CivPhys is a compact mechanics lens for reasoning about changing reality. It is not
a separate world model and never outranks direct evidence.

## CivPhys Primitives

Use these primitives only where they improve explanation:

- **Potential** — stored capability, options, reserves, or reachable future state.
- **Pressure** — demands, constraints, incentives, gradients, risks, or forces that
  create directional change.
- **Flow** — movement of resources, information, energy, money, attention, people,
  authority, or capability.
- **Membrane** — a boundary governing what may cross, under what conditions, with
  what authority and consequences.

CivPhys should explain a Bearing or constraint, not manufacture narrative over
unresolved evidence.

## Asset in Context

A useful contextual projection is:

Entity + spacetime + relationships + capabilities + affordances + constraints +
authority + evidence.

This is a view over canonical records, not another entity hierarchy.

## Membranes

A Membrane may independently govern data, authority, events, state, errors,
capabilities, identity, retention, and disclosure.

Adapters translate interfaces. Membranes govern crossing. One implementation may do
both, but the responsibilities remain distinct.

Blackwall is a high-assurance Membrane policy for boundaries requiring unusually
strict reachability, disclosure, or authority constraints. It is not a parallel
security architecture.

## Context Bridges

Retrieval, reasoning, retention, and disclosure are separate scopes.

Cross-domain reasoning should normally use a temporary task-scoped Context Bridge
instead of permanently merging domains. Prefer reconstructable information over
persisted sensitive derived state when continuity does not require the derivation.

A Query Budget limits retrieval to information capable of affecting the answer or
action. Retrievability alone is not justification for model context.

## Scoped Authority

Authority and responsibility are distinct and should be paired explicitly.

Participant -> authorized_to -> Capability -> over Resource/Entity -> inside
Membrane -> during Scope/Time.

Prefer narrow explicit authority over broad global roles. Delegation preserves
provenance, scope, duration, and revocation. Orphan responsibility and authority
gaps are diagnosable failures.

## Evidence Resolution

A provider is not truth merely because it is confident. Independent observations
remain independently attributable and may be resolved by an appropriate maintained
policy such as threshold, weighted confidence, unanimity, first-success, or
risk-adjusted selection.

Resolution never erases provenance or uncertainty.

## Relationship to Wayfinder

ARK preserves observations and evidence. CivPhys helps interpret forces and flows.
Membranes govern crossing. Authority bounds action. Bearings compile navigational
consequence. Jarvis presents and routes that consequence.
