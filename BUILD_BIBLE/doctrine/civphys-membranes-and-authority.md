# CivPhys, Membranes, and Authority

CivPhys is a compact mechanics lens for reasoning about changing reality. It is not
a separate world model and must not outrank direct evidence.

## CivPhys Primitives

Use four primitives where they improve explanation:

- Potential — stored capability, options, reserves, or reachable future state.
- Pressure — demands, constraints, incentives, gradients, risks, or forces that
  create directional change.
- Flow — movement of resources, information, energy, money, attention, people,
  authority, or capability.
- Membrane — a boundary that governs what may cross, under what conditions, with
  what authority and consequences.

CivPhys is explanatory. It should clarify why a Bearing changed or where useful
pressure remains, not manufacture narrative over unresolved evidence.

## Asset in Context

A useful contextual projection is:

Entity + spacetime + relationships + capabilities + affordances + constraints +
authority + evidence.

This is a projection over canonical records, not a reason to proliferate special
asset types. The same entity may have different relevant context for maintenance,
security, finance, construction, navigation, or household operation.

## Architectural Membranes

A Membrane controls permitted flow across a boundary. It may govern independently:

- data;
- authority;
- events;
- state;
- errors;
- capabilities;
- identity;
- retention; and
- disclosure.

Adapters translate interfaces. Membranes govern crossing. Do not collapse these
responsibilities merely because one implementation can perform both.

## Inference Boundary and Context Bridges

Retrieval scope, reasoning scope, retention scope, and disclosure scope are
separate controls.

Cross-domain reasoning should normally use a temporary, task-scoped Context Bridge
rather than permanently merging domains. Prefer reconstructable information over
persisted sensitive derived state when the derived state is not itself required for
continuity.

A Query Budget should limit retrieval to information capable of affecting the
answer or action. The existence of retrievable information is not a reason to pull
it into model context.

## Scoped Authority

Authority and responsibility are distinct dimensions and should be paired
explicitly.

Canonical authority relation:

Participant -> authorized_to -> Capability -> over Resource/Entity -> inside
Membrane -> during Scope/Time.

Avoid global roles such as "admin" when narrower, explicit authority is adequate.
Delegation must preserve provenance, scope, duration, and revocation. Orphan
responsibility and authority gaps are diagnosable system failures.

## Trust and Consensus

No single provider is automatically truth merely because it produced a confident
answer. Independent observations may remain independent evidence and be resolved by
a maintained policy such as threshold, weighted confidence, unanimity,
first-success, or risk-adjusted selection when appropriate.

Consensus does not erase provenance or uncertainty.

## Blackwall Reconciliation

Blackwall is not a second security universe. It is a high-assurance Membrane policy
for boundaries requiring unusually strict reachability, disclosure, or authority
constraints.

The general Membrane primitive remains canonical; Blackwall is a specialized
policy profile.

## Relationship to Wayfinder

ARK preserves observations and evidence. CivPhys helps interpret forces and flows.
Membranes govern crossing. Authority determines who or what may act. Bearings
compile the navigational consequence. Jarvis presents and routes that consequence.
