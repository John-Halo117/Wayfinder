# Legal Primitive Refactor Doctrine

Status: proposed stable semantic doctrine
Authority: Wayfinder
Scope: all law, regulation, adjudication, guidance, enforcement, and policy simulation

## Telos

Prefer legal and institutional states in which consequential rules are legible, causally justified, corrigible, and difficult to game; arbitrary cliffs, hidden denominators, ambiguous definitions, opaque defaults, accidental liability edges, and unpriced externalities are minimized when doing so improves the actual protected objectives.

This is an end-state criterion, not a mandate for any specific reform. A mechanism such as a 32-hour overtime threshold, portable benefits, license portability, a changed eligibility rule, or a different burden of proof remains a candidate intervention until evidence shows that it advances the relevant Teloi better than available alternatives.

## Primitive Types

Treat the following as first-class legal primitives whenever a change can propagate beyond the local text:

- definition and classification
- numeric threshold, floor, cap, band, or cliff
- denominator, population boundary, or unit of account
- eligibility boundary
- default or presumption
- burden or standard of proof
- jurisdiction, venue, standing, preemption, or delegation
- cause of action, remedy, liability edge, safe harbor, exception, or exemption
- time boundary, waiting period, limitation period, sunset, or trigger
- geographic or residency boundary
- valuation, accounting, aggregation, attribution, or ownership rule
- indexation or automatic adjustment rule
- verification or documentation requirement
- enforcement trigger or penalty function
- mandatory/discretionary operator such as may/shall

## Foundational Legal Delta

A Foundational Legal Delta is a change to a legal primitive whose downstream consequences are large relative to the apparent textual change.

Canonical causal chain:

`current legal state -> primitive delta -> directly affected population -> changed incentives/constraints -> behavioral response -> substitution/adaptation -> second-order externalities -> equilibrium effects -> distribution -> observed evidence`

Do not skip links merely because the proposal is popular, simple, or directionally aligned with a Telos.

## Legal Leverage

Legal leverage is multidimensional. A useful screening model may consider:

- affected population and denominator
- dependency centrality
- magnitude of the primitive delta
- behavioral elasticity or plausible response range
- propagation depth
- persistence
- enforceability
- implementation friction
- reversibility and option value
- gaming/arbitrage surface
- distributional concentration

Uncertainty is carried separately. Do not convert low confidence into false numerical precision or hide it inside a single score.

A scalar score may be used for triage only when the components are commensurable enough for the decision. Preserve non-dominated tradeoffs otherwise.

## Evaluation Gates

A candidate legal reform must distinguish objective from mechanism and pass the following checks before promotion:

1. **Objective gate** — name the desired state, protected constraints, and affected population independently of the proposed mechanism.
2. **Quantity gate** — preserve quantity semantics. Headcount, hours, wages, income, output, prices, stocks, flows, rates, and cumulative totals are not interchangeable.
3. **Counterfactual gate** — compare current law, no-change, the candidate delta, and materially distinct alternatives.
4. **Mechanism gate** — state how the legal primitive changes incentives, constraints, information, authority, prices, or permitted actions.
5. **Externality gate** — include substitution, displaced effects, second-order effects, feedbacks, offshoring/automation where relevant, administrative burden, and fraud/gaming.
6. **Distribution gate** — identify who gains, who loses, who bears transition cost, and which population denominator each claim uses.
7. **Institution gate** — test state capacity, federalism, competition, liberty, due process, enforceability, and implementation complexity where material.
8. **Evidence gate** — preserve observation, estimate, inference, forecast, assumption, preference, and contested claim as distinct epistemic types.
9. **Reversibility gate** — specify rollout, rollback, sunset, pilot, or reevaluation triggers proportional to consequence and uncertainty.
10. **Reality gate** — retire, revise, or reverse the reform when observed effects falsify the causal model or a superior intervention appears.

Legal simplicity is instrumental, not terminal. A simpler rule that worsens the protected objectives fails.

## Primitive Watch

Reality implementations should continuously detect high-leverage textual or interpretive deltas, including:

- changed numbers, thresholds, formulas, or denominators
- definition additions, removals, inclusions, and exclusions
- may/shall and mandatory/discretionary changes
- presumptions and burden shifts
- safe-harbor, exemption, exception, and waiver changes
- eligibility and verification changes
- jurisdiction, preemption, standing, remedy, and liability changes
- fixed values becoming indexed or indexed values becoming fixed
- aggregation, attribution, residency, ownership, or classification changes
- enforcement triggers, penalty schedules, and limitation periods

Detection must cover the complete legal state machine:

`idea -> proposal -> draft -> bill -> amendment -> enacted law -> proposed rule -> final rule -> guidance -> enforcement practice -> litigation -> injunction -> precedent -> implementation -> observed effects`

A change in effective law may occur without a statutory text change, so interpretation and enforcement are first-class events.

## Candidate Reform Portfolio

The following are seed candidates, not canonical truths. Each must be evaluated under the gates above and may be rejected or modified:

- lower standard-workweek/overtime thresholds, including 32-hour scenarios
- portable and continuously prorated benefits
- removal or smoothing of arbitrary benefit cliffs
- broader overtime coverage where total-system effects justify it
- occupational-license portability and removal of unnecessary licensing barriers
- skills-based alternatives to degree requirements
- worker-mobility and noncompete reform
- no-poach restrictions and labor-market competition measures
- pay transparency and information-symmetry reforms
- predictable scheduling and compensation for employer-controlled availability
- appropriate compensation for mandatory training and work-related travel
- clearer worker-status, joint-employer, franchise, staffing, and subcontracting boundaries
- reduction of classification arbitrage and misclassification incentives
- wage-floor simplification or indexation where the feedback rule dominates ad hoc adjustment
- training and skill-formation incentives
- technology rules that distinguish worker augmentation from pure displacement when that distinction changes welfare
- explicit, machine-readable definitions, denominators, defaults, burdens, triggers, effective dates, provenance, and dependency links

## Cross-System Inheritance

- **Teloi** owns desired states and protected constraints; it must not canonize a favored mechanism merely because it is familiar.
- **Reality** owns observed state, causal evidence, affected populations, propagation graphs, and post-implementation measurement.
- **Decision systems** own counterfactual comparison and preserve non-dominated tradeoffs.
- **Groundskeeper/operationalization implementations** should enforce the evaluation gates, detect missing causal links, prevent objective/mechanism collapse, and require reevaluation triggers.
- **News/proposal tracking** should rank foundational deltas by plausible leverage and uncertainty rather than headline salience alone.

Promote a specific reform into durable doctrine only when repeated evidence establishes the underlying principle at a higher abstraction. Keep implementation choices below that layer.
