# Wayfinder Navigation Primitives

Wayfinder is a navigation system, not an optimization system. It helps humans
continue, navigate, and improve reality without unnecessarily losing future options.
Applications, platforms, models, protocols, and harvested capabilities may supply
observations, execution, state, or interfaces; they do not replace this navigation
grammar.

## Root Objectives

Wayfinder evaluates architecture and action through four cross-cutting lenses:

- **Capability** — What can we do?
- **Continuity** — What survives and can be resumed?
- **Attention** — What still requires the human?
- **Maneuverability** — What useful future paths remain available?

These lenses do not replace Teloi. Teloi define mission direction; the root
objectives test how well architecture and action support it.

Maneuverability is a long-horizon governor, not an excuse for permanent indecision.

## Theseus Principle

Preserve the invariant. Replace the rest.

Capabilities, continuity, objectives, and required behavior may remain stable while
components, providers, implementations, protocols, and substrates change. A
replacement succeeds when the invariant survives, not when the old implementation
does.

## Capability Lifecycle

Acquire -> Observe -> Preserve -> Upgrade -> Transfer.

A durable capability should be obtainable, observable, preservable, improvable, and
transferable across appropriate substrate or provider changes.

## Bearings

Bearings provide orientation. They are not objectives. Metrics, scores, and
indicators may support a Bearing but must not become goals merely because they are
measurable.

Navigation seeks healthy operating regions rather than asymptotes. More certainty,
resilience, automation, optionality, knowledge, or capability is not automatically
better after its marginal navigational value falls below its cost or begins to
create new pathologies.

### Bearing Families

**Capability:** Capability Index, Effective Capability Yield, Upgrade Potential,
Transferability.

**Continuity:** Capsule Density, Continuity Recovery Velocity, Context Restoration
Capability, Labor Freedom Index.

**Attention:** Ambient Certainty, Bearings Availability, Bearing Velocity, Attention
Independence.

**Maneuverability:** Runway, Optionality, Affordance Expansion, Financial Freedom.

**Resource recovery:** Resource Recovery Efficiency, Capability Recapture Rate.

**Upgrade:** Upgrade Readiness, Capability Evolution Rate.

These are optional navigation instruments, not mandatory dashboard widgets.

## Color State and Certainty

Operational Color State:

- **Blue** — surplus or opportunity.
- **Green** — healthy or on course.
- **Yellow** — pressure requiring attention.
- **Red** — critical condition or failure.

Epistemic certainty is separate:

- **White** — adequately established; normally implicit.
- **Gray** — uncertain, stale, conflicting, or low-confidence.
- **Black** — unknown or unresolved.

Black is epistemic only; it is not a fifth operational severity. Unknown is a valid
state. When a responsible Bearing cannot be established, accept the unknown when it
does not matter, reduce it when justified, or preserve options until evidence
improves.

## Ambient Certainty and Bearings Economy

Ambient Certainty removes recurring uncertainty from human attention without
requiring active checking. Automation is a means; reduced recurring uncertainty is
the capability.

Bearings Economy accounts for the cost of maintaining orientation: checking,
context switching, interpreting, remembering, deciding, and rechecking.

Jarvis should reduce orientation cost as aggressively as direct task cost. The aim
is not more notifications; it is fewer unresolved attention loops.

## Expected Uncertainty Reduction

Expected Uncertainty Reduction (EUR) compares consequential uncertainty removed by
an intervention with its cost, time, attention, risk, and reversibility.

Use it when deciding whether another sensor, inspection, research task, experiment,
purchase, upgrade, or refactor would materially improve a decision. Stop when more
certainty would not change the course.

## Parallel Observation

Humans observe serially; Jarvis can observe in parallel. Many independent streams
may feed one finite Bearings stream. Parallel observation exists to reduce human
observation burden, not increase information volume.

## Affordance Projection and Opportunity

Wayfinder asks both what changed and what became possible.

Affordance Projection identifies newly reachable actions created by changed reality
or capability. Opportunity Detection filters those affordances through alignment,
actionability, reachability, relevance, and mission consequence.

Serendipity is allowed as affordance discovery. Novelty is not entitled to
attention.

## Goal and Pattern to Capability

Automation is downstream of capability:

Goal -> Capability -> Automation

Observed Pattern -> Capability Gap -> Automation.

A repeated action is not automatically a reason to automate it. Identify the
capability or certainty gap first, then choose the least bespoke adequate
intervention.

## Navigation Flywheel

Uncertainty Reduced
-> Attention Recovered
-> Better Bearings
-> Better Decisions
-> Better Reality
-> More Capability
-> More Opportunities
-> More Uncertainty Reduced.

Continuity and Context Restoration are defined in
[Continuity and Capsules](continuity-and-capsules.md); this doctrine consumes them
rather than redefining them.

## Jarvis Functions

Jarvis is the human-facing navigation layer. Its durable functions include:

- Bearings — what changed and what matters now;
- Opportunity Detection — what became possible;
- Risk Detection — what threatens continuity or a path;
- Affordance Projection — what actions are newly available;
- Context Restoration — how to continue from here;
- Resource Recovery — what existing flows can become capability;
- Upgrade Discovery — what bottleneck or capability should improve next; and
- Ambient Certainty — what recurring uncertainty can leave human attention.

Systems implement these functions. No application is the function itself.

## Jarvis Operating Modes

- **Ambient** — quiet observation and finite surfacing of meaningful change.
- **Conversation** — ordinary dialogue.
- **Whiteboard** — exploratory synthesis and design.
- **Mission** — focused pursuit of a defined objective.
- **Debug** — diagnosis, traces, contradictions, failures, and system state.
- **Socratic** — deliberate questioning to expose assumptions and improve a model
  or decision.

Modes change interaction posture, not constitutional authority or identity.

## Capability Injection Test

Evaluate technology by what capability it injects into Observation, Interpretation,
Reasoning, Navigation, Continuity, Maneuverability, Experience Density, or
Legibility. Technology that materially improves none of them should not gain
architectural weight merely because it is impressive.

## Canonical Compression

ARK preserves reality. Capsules preserve continuity. Jarvis preserves Bearings.
Wayfinder converts evidence and uncertainty into actionable capability while
minimizing the attention required to navigate reality.
