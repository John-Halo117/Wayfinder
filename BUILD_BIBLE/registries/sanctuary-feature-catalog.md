# Sanctuary Concrete Feature Catalog

Purpose: preserve concrete physical things explicitly discussed for Sanctuary
without turning each item into doctrine or a universal requirement.

This is the canonical physical feature list for the current GH/MH/Campus
assumption cleanup. Polaris/System implementations may mirror these entries for
validation or projection but do not own their physical meaning.

Every item is one of:

- **available** — may be used when it satisfies the requirement;
- **preferred** — current profile default when adequate;
- **conditional** — use only when the stated tradeoff earns it;
- **campus-preferred** — normally evaluate outside residential interior space;
- **rejected** — obsolete under the current one-story GH/MH constraints.

A listed feature is not automatically a requirement.

## Use Rules

1. Resolve the requirement before selecting a feature.
2. Resolve the building profile before inheriting an old recommendation.
3. `UNKNOWN_SCOPE` historical specifics remain quarantined until reconciled.
4. Search applicable standards/mature interoperable interfaces before custom physical interfaces.
5. A mechanism must earn its deployment, reset, maintenance, failure, clearance, and replacement burden.
6. A feature that is available is not automatically installed.
7. Essential daily capability remains accessible.
8. GH and MH remain one human-occupied story; elevated storage/cat/service volume is not an occupied second story.
9. Preserve exact user-specified concrete items even when a higher-level generalization also exists; generalization does not erase the specific.
