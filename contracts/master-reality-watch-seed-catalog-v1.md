# Master Reality Watch Seed Catalog v1

Status: canonical seed catalog for the Master Reality Watch Registry.

This file preserves concrete user-specified things Polaris should know are watchable. It is intentionally **not closed**: new useful observables/proxies append to the registry without creating new subsystems.

## 1. Land & Ecology

Ecoregions; watersheds; wetlands; forests; canopy; habitat; biodiversity; invasive species; species observations; conservation status; fragmentation; land conversion; soil; groundwater; rivers; reservoirs; drought; fire/fuels; pollution; restoration activity; conservation activity.

## 2. Climate & Physical Environment

Temperature; humidity/dew point; precipitation; wind; storms; snow; smoke; air quality; solar exposure; night sky; noise; geological hazards; erosion; flooding; compound hazards.

## 3. Housing & Real Estate

Prices; rents; inventory; days on market; price cuts; repeat cuts; delist/relist; concessions; sale/list ratios; listings below acquisition basis; foreclosures; tax delinquency; liens; vacancies; permits; demolitions; builder incentives; seller pressure; buyer leverage; distress.

## 4. Development & Land Use

Zoning; rezonings; plats; parcelization; annexations; permits; grading; road cuts; utility extensions; subdivisions; commercial construction; industrial development; data centers; transmission; pipelines; conservation easements.

## 5. Population & Demography

Population; age structure; households; births/deaths; migration; school enrollment; vacancies; address churn; utility-account counts; vehicle registrations; other qualified migration proxies.

## 6. Economy & Cost of Living

Employment; wages; openings; layoffs; business formation/closure; bankruptcies; commercial vacancy; retail activity; prices; food; energy; housing burden; taxes; household purchasing power.

## 7. Physical Commerce

Truck traffic; rail activity; ports; warehouses; freight rates; container flows; inventories; shortages; parking occupancy; restaurant activity; retail footfall; commercial waste.

## 8. Infrastructure

Roads; bridges; traffic; transit; water; sewer; electric grid; telecom; broadband; outages; repair frequency; deferred maintenance; capacity additions; infrastructure condition.

## 9. Government & Civic Capacity

Budgets; revenues; debt; bonds; procurement; staffing; vacancies; response times; capital projects; emergency contracts; equipment purchases/auctions; regulatory changes.

## 10. Agriculture & Food Systems

Crops; livestock; harvests; disease; fertilizer; hay/feed; irrigation; slaughter; commodity flows; farm economics; farmland conversion; local food capacity.

## 11. Health & Emergency Capacity

Aggregate hospital/ER pressure; EMS volume; ambulance response; shortages; diversion reporting; public-health signals; emergency-system capacity. Keep this aggregate/place/system level rather than private-person tracking.

## 12. Insurance & Physical Risk Pricing

Premiums; filings; nonrenewals; insurer exits/entries; residual/FAIR-plan participation; claims aggregates; deductibles; insurability.

## 13. Mobility & Accessibility

Travel times; congestion; closures; fuel prices; airport activity; road quality; freight interference; actual accessibility of important destinations.

## 14. Public Safety & Disorder

Calls for service; response times; incident patterns; abandoned vehicles; code complaints; fires; other aggregate place-level signals. Do not build private-person dossiers.

## 15. Tourism & Transient Pressure

Hotels; short-term rentals; campground utilization; park visitation; airport passengers; traffic; reservations; events; seasonal population loading.

## 16. Culture & Community

Institutions; churches; clubs; libraries; local events; independent businesses; closures/openings; other measurable community infrastructure.

## 17. Technology & Capability Ratchets

Mandates; surveillance infrastructure; connected-device requirements; repairability; right-to-repair; offline capability; interoperability; ownership restrictions; disappearance/reappearance of low-tech alternatives.

## 18. Conservation & National Stewardship

Protected acreage; acquisitions; easements; public-land changes; ecological restoration; watershed protection; extraction/development pressure; threats/help affecting nationally or locally important natural systems.

## 19. Great Rebasement

Demographic turnover; retiree concentration; estate-driven asset turnover; housing release; commercial repricing; municipal fiscal effects; land-use transition; downstream geographic redistribution.

## 20. Sanctuary / Property External Twin

Every externally generated condition that materially changes desirability, resilience, privacy, cost, accessibility, or capability of a candidate/owned property.

## Proxy & Exhaust seed watches

These are indirect signals and require an explicit mechanism, spatial/temporal validity, confidence, and confounder treatment before they inform state:

- storage prices -> displacement/moving pressure;
- dumpster permits -> renovation/turnover;
- school-bus routes -> settlement/change in served population geography;
- well permits/completions -> groundwater/development pressure;
- sewer contracts/extensions -> development/capacity pressure;
- government equipment purchases -> anticipated or changing operational demand/capacity;
- government/industry equipment auctions -> contraction, replacement cycle, or fleet turnover;
- insurance filings -> repricing of physical risk/insurability;
- price-cut cascades/repeat cuts -> seller pressure;
- mosquito treatment -> qualified hydrological/ecological conditions;
- road-salt/deicer consumption -> realized winter road burden;
- utility connections/accounts -> occupied-development realization;
- culvert-pipe purchases/contracts -> qualified drainage/road/infrastructure workload where procurement context supports it.

No proxy implication is automatic. Each arrow is a candidate mechanism to validate, not a fact by naming convention.

## Standing extension rule

Whenever a weird, useful, reasonably obtainable signal of consequential reality is discovered:

`TACK IT ON -> QUALIFY -> CONNECT TO STATE -> COMPOSE -> KEEP QUIET UNLESS MATERIAL`

The list may become enormous. Human-facing output should become smaller, not larger.
