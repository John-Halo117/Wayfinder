# Polaris Constitutional Invariants v2

Status: CANONICAL / ARCHITECTURE-LEVEL
Supersedes v1 by extension; direction is anchored, implementation remains corrigible.

## Prime law
ANCHOR THE DIRECTION, NOT THE IMPLEMENTATION. Reality, changed requirements, safety, law, authority, or better evidence may recompile any mechanism.

## Constitutional gradients
REALITY > MODEL CONSISTENCY; EVENTS/DELTAS > REPEATED SNAPSHOTS; EVIDENCE > CLAIMS; MORE CAPABILITY > MORE MACHINERY; LESS HUMAN ATTENTION > MORE HUMAN-VISIBLE INFORMATION; SHARED SEMANTICS > DUPLICATION; INHERIT + STORE DELTA > SHADOW COPY; DERIVED/REHYDRATABLE STATE > UNNECESSARY PERSISTED STATE; PASSIVE/STATIC/DETERMINISTIC > ACTIVE/MECHANICAL/SOFTWARE/MODEL when decision-equivalent; EXISTING STANDARD/COMMODITY INTERFACE > CUSTOM; REVERSIBLE/REPLACEABLE PAYLOAD > PERMANENT OBLIGATION; LOCAL/INCREMENTAL CHANGE > GLOBAL RECOMPUTATION; COMPOSITION/EXISTING PRIMITIVE > NEW SUBSYSTEM; SILENCE > NON-ACTIONABLE OUTPUT; SIMPLE ADEQUATE MECHANISM > CLEVER MECHANISM; WHOLE-SYSTEM COST > LOCAL METRIC WIN; ACCESS/SERVICE/RECOVERY > CONCEALED FRAGILITY; HUMAN SOVEREIGNTY/AUTHORITY BOUNDARY > AUTOMATION CONVENIENCE.

## Hard conservation invariants
1. Requirement sovereignty: mechanisms never manufacture requirements.
2. One truth, many views: canonical state exists once; apps/reports/Jarvis/Wayfinder/nodes are projections.
3. One observation, many uses: acquire/canonicalize once; derive many legitimate views.
4. Semantic conservation: compression/dedup may remove representation, never consequential distinctions.
5. Epistemic conservation: observation != inference != estimate != prediction != assumption != unknown; transformations cannot silently promote epistemic strength.
6. Quantity conservation: stock/flow/rate/cumulative state, units, numerator/denominator and population boundaries survive transformation.
7. Causal conservation: correlation/proxy/composition never silently becomes causation.
8. Boundary conservation: scope, authority, geography, time, population and objective boundaries travel with state.
9. Dependency locality: local change awakens only reachable dependents.
10. Failure locality: failure remains within the smallest legitimate blast radius where feasible.
11. Authority non-amplification: composition/delegation cannot create authority no principal possessed.
12. Commitment proportionality: evidence/verification burden rises with consequence x irreversibility.
13. Optionality preservation: cheap future options are not consumed without compensating present value.
14. Permanent/replaceable separation: long-lived skeletons/interfaces do not inherit unnecessary assumptions from short-lived payloads.
15. Standards-first: adequate existing standard -> mature commodity/open interface -> composition/adaptation -> custom unresolved delta only.
16. Custom-surface minimization: when custom is necessary, minimize the proprietary boundary.
17. Complexity must delete complexity: new complexity preferably removes greater lifecycle complexity elsewhere.
18. Automation must delete administration: automation that increases babysitting/configuration/exception burden fails unless net administration falls.
19. Intelligence must delete attention: improved models should reduce human cognition required for decision-equivalent outcomes.
20. Capability without occupancy: intermittent capability does not automatically earn permanent space/state/resources.
21. Cheap reserve before speculative hardware: preserve space/routes/access/power/standard interfaces before future-proofing equipment.
22. Boring baseline permanence: every optimization competition retains a simple proven baseline.
23. Whole-system savings only: savings exist only after propagated/rebound costs are reconciled.
24. No savings double counting: one removed burden enters the ledger once even when many causal paths explain it.
25. Deferred != saved: delayed capital remains an obligation until eliminated.
26. Avoided tail risk != expected savings: risk reduction remains distinct from invoice reduction.
27. Physical balance first: for conserved/throughput systems establish stocks, inputs, outputs, transformations, losses and reuse before optimization.
28. Event primacy: store transitions where state is reconstructible rather than unchanged snapshots.
29. Recoverability over materialization: preserve sufficient events/evidence/provenance to reconstruct; do not persist every derivative.
30. Information-value admission: acquisition/storage/compute/bandwidth/maintenance/attention must earn themselves through consequential information value and reuse.
31. Resolution follows decision: do not acquire/process/display finer resolution than can affect action unless cheap preservation has option value.
32. Uncertainty stop condition: resolve uncertainty only until the remainder cannot materially change action or protected constraints.
33. Contradiction is information: independent disagreement is preserved until discriminated, not averaged away.
34. Negative space is a resource: unused spatial/computational/cognitive/organizational capacity may be valuable slack.
35. Slack is not inefficiency: preserve justified capacity for variability, recovery and maneuverability.
36. Interface count is a cost: modularity/sharing wins only when interface burden is lower than coupling removed.
37. No universal interface: standardize common semantics without forcing unrelated requirements through one abstraction.
38. Specialization is delta-shaped: domain children contain only mechanics genuinely absent from ancestors.
39. Inheritance remains lossless: factoring upward cannot erase meaningful child differences.
40. Naming has a cost: stable useful concepts earn names; transient patterns do not automatically become nouns.
41. Documentation follows reality: executable contracts/state/receipts generate documentation where possible; prose must not become an independent truth fork.
42. Tests protect intent: regression tests cover invariants/failure modes, not only current mechanisms.
43. Replacement includes removal: replacement is incomplete while superseded authoritative machinery remains without reason.
44. No orphan state: every persisted state has owner, provenance/derivation, lifecycle and invalidation rule.
45. No orphan capability: every capability maps upward to objective/requirement or is challenged.
46. No orphan sensor: every active sensor discriminates consequential uncertainty/state.
47. No orphan metric: every metric names its property and decision relevance.
48. No orphan automation: every automation has objective, authority, stop condition, failure behavior and escalation boundary.
49. No orphan physical feature: every component states its requirement and deletion consequence.
50. Silence is valid output: successful monitoring often produces no human message.
51. Closure beats activity: success is verified objective state, not work performed.
52. Maintenance is part of purchase: acquisition includes service, replacement, updates, cleaning, inspection and disposal.
53. Recovery is part of design: capability without credible failure/recovery behavior is incomplete.
54. Reality recompiles parents: material child changes propagate upward; obsolete parent conclusions are not preserved for consistency.
55. Optimization has a stop condition: stop when marginal work no longer materially improves objective, confidence, lifecycle burden, optionality or action.

## Cross-layer non-evasion law
Burden cannot disappear by changing layers. Moving complexity from physical -> digital, software -> user, capital -> maintenance, storage -> bandwidth, automation -> supervision, or local -> shared does not count as deletion. Account at the whole-system boundary.

## Decompression invariant
Every consequential abstraction owes a recoverable path:
PITHY STATE -> WHY -> COMPONENTS -> EVIDENCE -> ORIGINAL OBSERVATION.
Human projection may compress brutally; consequential meaning must remain recoverable.

## Recompilation invariant
Every claimed optimization must survive:
LOCAL WIN -> DEPENDENTS -> PARENTS -> LIFECYCLE -> HUMAN STATE.
Only then may it become a verified optimization receipt/default ratchet.

## Human-interface invariant
Default projection: WHAT CHANGED -> DIRECTION -> CONSEQUENCE/ACTION. Stable background collapses. Expand only when it can change understanding, confidence, choice or action, or when explicitly requested. Material uncertainty/contradiction overrides silence.

## Optimization order
DELETE -> SIMPLIFY -> STANDARDIZE -> SHARE/INHERIT -> REUSE -> PASSIVE -> AUTOMATE NECESSARY WORK -> OPTIMIZE SURVIVORS. Machine optimization follows semantic/work elimination.

## No-new-noun gate
A proposed subsystem/domain/engine/registry first attempts representation as instance, delta, relation, operator, projection, adapter, composition or existing primitive. New nouns require unresolved requirements plus demonstrated inadequacy of existing shapes.

## Conservation ledger
Account material deltas to ATTENTION; COMPLEXITY; PERSISTED STATE; DEPENDENCIES; PERMANENT OBLIGATIONS; RESOURCE/CAPITAL; AUTHORITY; FAILURE SURFACE; RECOVERY; MAINTENANCE; REVERSIBILITY; OPTIONALITY; HUMAN ADMINISTRATION; INTERFACE COUNT; CUSTOM SURFACE; BANDWIDTH/COMPUTE/STORAGE where consequential.

## Optimization receipt
Record objective/protected constraints; burden removed; mechanism deleted/simplified/replaced; resulting invariant/default; assumptions/boundaries; evidence/verification; propagated parent/lifecycle result; and reversal trigger.

## Ratchet with Reality escape hatch
Verified simplifications become defaults. Reintroduction of removed burden carries proof. Changed reality may justify reversal.

## Polar failure modes
Constitution worship; frozen architecture; metric bureaucracy; receipt spam; deletion fetish; silence hiding uncertainty; passive/standards dogma; local simplicity exporting global complexity; under-modeling reality; treating all complexity as bad; refusing justified specialization/new primitives; false canonical truth; lossy inheritance; overcentralization; optionality hoarding; slack bloat; proof burden so high that correction stalls.
